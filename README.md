## ◈ Executive Synopsis

I architect **production-grade platforms** at the confluence of **product strategy**, **distributed systems**, and **human-centered growth**.

As **Founder & CEO of [Ads Platform](https://adsplatform.net)** (via **ByteLabs**), I ship AI-augmented advertising infrastructure that unlocks the Telegram Ads ecosystem — automation pipelines, performance optimization, and multi-tenant SaaS serving **tens of thousands of active operators**.

I also build **[IranRouter](https://iranrouter.net)** — a unified AI gateway that abstracts GPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek and beyond behind a single, payment-frictionless API surface.

> *I don't ship features. I ship decision systems that compound.*
